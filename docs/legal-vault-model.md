## 1. Business Model Summary

**Model:** Retail gold sale + free optional vault storage (custodian service)

```
Customer buys physical gold at market price
→ Kasih Gold Easy stores it FREE in insured vault
→ Customer tracks holdings via digital dashboard
→ Customer can redeem as physical plates (0.2g min) or sell back at daily price
```

This is the same model used by **Kasih AP Gold (KAP Gold)**, **Public Gold**, and
**Quantum Metal** — all operating under SSM + Premise license only.

| Trigger for SC/BNM License | Kasih Gold Easy Model |

## 3. Four Non-Negotiable Legal Pillars

These must be clearly stated in T&C, onboarding, and marketing:

### Pillar 1 — Customer Owns Physical Gold
> Customer retains full legal ownership of their gold at all times.
> Kasih Gold Easy holds gold as custodian, not as owner.

### Pillar 2 — Free Storage as Customer Service
> Vault storage is a complimentary service offered to gold purchasers.
> It is not a financial product, deposit, or investment scheme.

### Pillar 3 — No Guaranteed Returns
> Gold price risk is borne entirely by the customer.
> Kasih Gold Easy makes no representations about future gold value.

### Pillar 4 — Audited, Insured Vault
> Gold is stored in insured (RM10 million), fireproof, audited vaults.
> Certificates of insurance and audit reports available on request.

## 5. Risk Boundaries — What Would Require Additional Licensing

Avoid these at all costs without proper licensing:

- Promising **fixed monthly returns** on gold holdings → triggers SC
- Accepting **cash/fiat deposits** before gold purchase → triggers BNM
- Pooling customer gold into a **collective fund** → triggers SC (CIS rules)
- Operating a **secondary market** for gold trading between customers → triggers SC
- Claiming to be a **gold investment platform** (use "gold savings" or "gold purchase" instead)

## 7. Shariah Compliance Notes (Ar Rahnu)

- Ar Rahnu is Islamic pawn broking — permissible under Shariah
- **Ujrah** (storage/safekeeping fee) replaces interest — not riba
- Gold must be **physically held** as collateral (marhun) — we hold it
- Financing based on **current market value**, not future price — no gharar
- No compounding charges — fixed ujrah per month per gram
- Structure reviewed and endorsed by Shariah advisory board
