# COLLABORATION AGREEMENT

**Between SLV Group and Adcellent Sdn Bhd**
**In respect of AiServe ESG OS Platform**

---

**Date:** 12 May 2026

**This Collaboration Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into between:

1. **SLV Group** (Registration No: [●]), a company incorporated in Malaysia having its registered address at [●] ("**SLV**"); and

2. **Adcellent Sdn Bhd** (Registration No: [●]), a company incorporated in Malaysia having its registered address at [●] ("**Adcellent**").

SLV and Adcellent are hereinafter individually referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**".

---

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**

A. SLV has developed and owns the **AiServe ESG OS** platform, a software-as-a-service solution providing ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) reporting tools for Malaysian SMEs and corporations.

B. Adcellent is engaged in business development and consulting services and has an established network for recruiting and managing Associate Partners in the professional services market.

C. The Parties wish to collaborate to commercialise the AiServe ESG OS platform through Adcellent's go-to-market network, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

---

## PART 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"Associate Partner"** means any individual or entity recruited by Adcellent to deliver ESG reporting services using the Platform.
- **"Base Report Price"** means **RM 8,000** per ESG report, being the price charged by Adcellent to Associate Partners.
- **"Client"** means any end-customer who engages an Associate Partner for ESG reporting services.
- **"Consultant Fee"** means 30% of the Base Report Price payable to designated consultants as described in Clause 5.
- **"ESG Report"** means a completed ESG sustainability report prepared using the Platform for a Client.
- **"IPO"** means an Initial Public Offering of shares on Bursa Malaysia or any recognised securities exchange.
- **"Platform"** means the AiServe ESG OS software, including all features, data, and infrastructure operated by SLV.
- **"SLV Fee"** means 15% of the Base Report Price as described in Clause 5.

---

## PART 2 — SCOPE OF COLLABORATION

**2.1** SLV shall:
  (a) Provide and maintain access to the Platform for Adcellent and all authorised Associate Partners;
  (b) Develop, update, and improve the Platform including ESG framework updates;
  (c) Provide technical support to Adcellent for platform-related issues;
  (d) Issue and manage platform login credentials for Associate Partners as directed by Adcellent.

**2.2** Adcellent shall:
  (a) Lead all go-to-market activities for the Platform in Malaysia;
  (b) Recruit, onboard, train, and manage Associate Partners;
  (c) Set the terms of engagement with Associate Partners;
  (d) Collect all fees from Associate Partners and remit SLV's share in accordance with Clause 5;
  (e) Market the Platform and ESG reporting services to prospective Clients and Associate Partners.

---

## PART 3 — ASSOCIATE PARTNER PROGRAMME

**3.1** Adcellent shall operate the Associate Partner Programme on the following commercial terms:

| Pricing Level | Amount |
|---|---|
| Client pays Associate (recommended) | RM 15,000 per report |
| Associate pays Adcellent (cost price) | RM 8,000 per report |
| Associate gross margin | RM 7,000 per report |

**3.2** Associate Partners may set their own client-facing prices. The recommended price of **RM 15,000 per ESG Report** is a guide only.

**3.3** Adcellent is solely responsible for the recruitment, vetting, training, and commercial conduct of Associate Partners.

---

## PART 4 — PLATFORM ACCESS & LICENCING

**4.1** SLV grants Adcellent and its authorised Associate Partners a **non-exclusive, non-transferable licence** to access and use the Platform solely for the purpose of preparing ESG Reports for Clients under this Agreement.

**4.2** Associate Partners shall not share login credentials, reverse-engineer the Platform, or use the Platform for any purpose outside of Client ESG reporting.

**4.3** SLV retains all rights, title, and interest in the Platform, including all intellectual property, source code, data models, and ESG indicator databases.

---

## PART 5 — REVENUE ALLOCATION

**5.1** Of each **Base Report Price of RM 8,000** collected by Adcellent per ESG Report delivered:

| Recipient | Percentage | Amount (RM) | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| SLV Group | 15% | RM 1,200 | IT Solutions & Platform Development Fee |
| Consultants | 30% | RM 2,400 | Consultant Service Fee |
| Adcellent | 55% | RM 4,400 | Operations, Growth & Management |
| **Total** | **100%** | **RM 8,000** | |

**5.2** The Consultant Fee of 30% (RM 2,400) shall be distributed by Adcellent to its designated consultants in accordance with Adcellent's internal remuneration policy. SLV has no obligation in respect of consultant fee payments.

**5.3 Payment to SLV:** Adcellent shall remit **RM 1,200 per completed ESG Report** to SLV within **thirty (30) days** of each report being delivered to a Client.

**5.4** Adcellent shall provide SLV with a monthly statement no later than the **5th day of each calendar month**, itemising the number of ESG Reports delivered in the preceding month and the corresponding fees due to SLV.

**5.5** All payments shall be made by bank transfer to SLV's designated bank account. Details to be provided separately in writing.

---

## PART 6 — IPO SHARE CONVERSION RIGHTS

**6.1** In consideration of SLV's platform contribution and ongoing development commitment, SLV shall be entitled to **Share Conversion Rights** upon a qualifying IPO on the following basis:

  (a) Upon a qualifying IPO by either Party, or a jointly established entity incorporating the commercial activities under this Agreement, SLV shall be entitled to convert its cumulative platform contribution into shares equivalent to **50% of the market value** of such contribution at the time of the IPO;

  (b) "Market value of SLV's contribution" shall be determined by reference to the IPO prospectus valuation, or failing agreement, by an independent valuer mutually appointed by the Parties;

  (c) The share conversion right is non-dilutable below the agreed percentage without SLV's prior written consent;

  (d) Both Parties commit to negotiate in good faith the precise share structure, vesting schedule, and lock-up period no later than **six (6) months prior** to any intended IPO filing.

**6.2** These rights shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years**, should an IPO occur within that period following termination.

---

## PART 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY

**7.1** Each Party agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information disclosed by the other Party, including but not limited to: platform source code, client lists, pricing, financial data, and business strategy.

**7.2** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for **three (3) years**.

**7.3** Confidentiality does not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was rightfully known prior to disclosure; or (c) is required to be disclosed by law or regulatory authority.

---

## PART 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**8.1** The Platform (including all code, designs, data structures, ESG indicators, and branding) is and shall remain the exclusive property of **SLV**.

**8.2** Adcellent's brand, client relationships, associate network, and go-to-market materials are and shall remain the exclusive property of **Adcellent**.

**8.3** Neither Party shall sub-license, assign, or transfer the other Party's intellectual property without prior written consent.

---

## PART 9 — EXCLUSIVITY & NON-COMPETE

**9.1** During the term of this Agreement:
  (a) Adcellent shall not engage, deploy, or promote any competing ESG reporting platform developed by a third party through its Associate Partner network without SLV's prior written consent;
  (b) SLV shall not appoint any alternative go-to-market partner in Malaysia for the associate-based ESG reporting model without Adcellent's prior written consent.

**9.2** These restrictions apply within Malaysia only.

---

## PART 10 — REPRESENTATIONS & WARRANTIES

**10.1** Each Party represents and warrants that:
  (a) It is duly incorporated and in good standing under Malaysian law;
  (b) It has full authority to enter into this Agreement;
  (c) Entry into this Agreement does not breach any other agreement to which it is party.

---

## PART 11 — TERM & TERMINATION

**11.1 Term:** This Agreement commences on the date of signing and continues for an initial period of **two (2) years**, and shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives **ninety (90) days' written notice** of non-renewal before the end of any term.

**11.2 Termination for Breach:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within **thirty (30) days** of receiving written notice of such breach.

**11.3 Effect of Termination:**
  (a) All outstanding payments due to SLV shall be remitted within 30 days of the termination date;
  (b) Adcellent shall cease using the Platform and direct Associate Partners to do the same;
  (c) SLV shall preserve Associate Partner data for 90 days post-termination to facilitate any transition;
  (d) IPO share conversion rights under Clause 6.2 shall survive as stated.

---

## PART 12 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**12.1** Neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, consequential, or loss of profit damages arising from this Agreement.

**12.2** SLV's total liability under this Agreement in any 12-month period shall not exceed the total SLV Fees received in that period.

---

## PART 13 — GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**13.1** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Malaysia**.

**13.2** Any dispute shall first be resolved through good-faith negotiation within **30 days** of written notice.

**13.3** If unresolved, the dispute shall be referred to mediation administered by the **Asian International Arbitration Centre (AIAC)** in Kuala Lumpur.

---

## PART 14 — GENERAL

**14.1 Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions, representations, and understandings.

**14.2 Amendments:** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

**14.3 Notices:** Notices shall be in writing and delivered by email (with read receipt) or registered post to the addresses stated on the signature page.

**14.4 Severability:** If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

**14.5 No Waiver:** Failure to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver of future enforcement rights.
